### ⚠️ Scope Clarification

A **complete** drum & bass database (1988-present) would require:
- **500-700+ artists** (we have ~280)
- **1,000+ interviews** (copyright-sensitive)
- **5,000+ quotes** (copyright-sensitive)
- **100+ labels** (we have 19)

**Phase 1 was deliberately scoped** to establish a solid foundation with verified data from Wikipedia and existing sources.
